Rate card (editable)

Only admins see this section. Changes are saved for all staff and apply at once. The two annual figures are the full-facility floor and ceiling; every quote is scaled from them.

How the price is built: a term score (1 day = 0, 1 week = 0.02, 2 weeks = 0.04, 1 month = 0.1, 3 = 0.35, 6 = 0.65, 9 = 0.85, 12 months = 1; the maximum term is 12 months and a month is counted as 30 days). The deposit is the full deposit (months of rent above) for stays of 3 months or more, and shrinks steadily to the short-stay percentage for stays of 2 weeks or less, with the minimum deposit as a floor and a space score (share of the facility, slightly favouring smaller shares) are blended by the weight above, plus any payment-plan bonus, capped at 1. The full-facility annual rate slides from the maximum (score 0) to the minimum (score 1). The customer pays their share of that rate.
